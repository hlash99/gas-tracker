Method & caveats

  • Fuel means gas-category charges of $20 or more. Smaller ones are car washes, snacks or top-offs, and ChargePoint is EV charging (about $7 per 30 days so far).
  • Porsche: it's filled only on weekends, so weekend fills from Feb 21 to Aug 23 2026 are left out of the Mini numbers. The Mini baselines use the 12 months before the Porsche and the same season a year earlier.
  • Confirmed by Hassan on Oct 8 2026: Sep 13 (Half Moon Bay) and Sep 20 were the Porsche, Sep 16 was the Civic, and Sep 9 and Oct 8 were the Volvo.
  • Prices: EIA is an area average. The Menlo Park Chevron runs above it, so the Volvo's gallons are probably slightly overstated, which makes these numbers conservative.
  • Not included: home charging. A full charge costs about $5 at PG&E EV2-A off-peak, so charging most nights would cancel much of the gas saving. Office charging is free.
  • Updating: new fills are added from Monarch by hand. EIA prices refresh every week on their own.
Data: Monarch · EIA weekly retail gasoline prices, San Francisco · Built Oct 2026.
